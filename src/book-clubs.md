The Life Out Of Reach series tells my story without protective distance or tidy resolutions. Most chapters pair a scene as I lived it with a reflective account. That structure invites readers to see the same experience differently, making these books ideal for honest book club conversations and difficult questions.

<h2><a href="/books/spare-burden/">Spare Burden</a></h2>

<p>Growing up as one of six daughters in a Sikh Punjabi family in 1980s Britain, I was taught that girls mattered less than boys. Spare Burden traces my childhood and adolescence, exploring love, duty, belonging and ambition. It asks what happens when a daughter questions the rules shaping her future.</p>

<h2><a href="/books//books/not-the-property-of/">Not The Property Of</a></h2>

<p>At sixteen, my parents arranged my engagement without asking me. Not The Property Of follows my struggle, between sixteen and nineteen, to keep my education and make my own choices. Secretly involved with a Muslim boy, I faced family pressure, an unwanted fiancé and a forced marriage I never chose.</p>
