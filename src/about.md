blockOne: |
  One of six daughters, I was raised to obey and put men first. Tradition, respectability and silence shaped me, then fuelled my refusal to conform. My writing welcomes generations from Sikh Punjabi households, British Asian immigrant families and anyone who has ever felt caught between cultures, seeking connection and belonging.

blockTwo: |
  My story is larger than surviving a forced marriage and coercive control. During my second marriage, I co-founded and helped build a successful company from the ground up, earning recognition in Who’s Who of Britain’s Business Elite, before being forced to surrender control of what I had helped create. My former husband later sold the company for a nominal $1 to an American billionaire. I chose freedom over financial security despite the profound personal and financial consequences of leaving. Today, I transform lived experience into literature that refuses to look away — tender where it can be and ruthless when it must be.

  My writing examines family systems, marriage, power, money, cultural pressure, institutional failure and the gradual reclamation of personal authority. I write not because I am confessional, but because I understand that testimony is a form of power. My seven-book memoir series, Life Out of Reach, traces a girlhood and womanhood negotiated between expectation and ambition, belonging and independence, obedience and self-preservation.

  Taken together, the series is more than my story. It examines what happens when culture, family, gender and silence combine to deny a girl authority over her own life — and what it takes for that woman to reclaim it. Across the series, I write in crisp, cinematic scenes — part witness statement, part love letter to the girls who were taught to keep their mouths shut and their heads down.

  My lived experience also informs my work as a divorce strategist. I understand that leaving a relationship is rarely only an emotional decision. It can involve children, housing, debt, businesses, legal systems, financial survival and the rebuilding of an identity. My work addresses the practical realities of separation and divorce, including financial independence and rebuilding a life afterwards.
