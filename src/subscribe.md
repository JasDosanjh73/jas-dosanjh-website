## The Ugly Duckling Quacks Substack

The Life Out of Reach series tells the story in seven books. But some stories sit just outside the pages and they’re still too important to be left untold. The Ugly Duckling Quacks is where that writing lives. Essays, reflections and pieces that go further into the world of the memoir: the questions it raises, the characters it carries and the truths that needed more room than a chapter could give.
Everyone who subscribes also receives occasional updates when a new book is published.

### Paddling: For people who want to stay close to the work (Free)

Two pieces of my writing a month. Occasional updates on new books as they publish.

<hr data-decoration="thin">

### Waddling: For readers who want to go deeper (£5.99 per month)

Six posts a month: two free essays plus four exclusive pieces, and private access to the world behind the memoir series. Bonus chapters. Previously unpublished scenes. Behind-the-scenes reflections. Character updates. Reader Q&As. The occasional video from that era. And the stories behind the stories, content that sits just outside the books, but still belongs to my story and widens the truth of some stories and characters that seemed minor in the book, but I can now expand their stories.
