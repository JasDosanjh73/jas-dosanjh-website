What happens when a woman decides her marriage is over, but everyone around her refuses to accept that she has the right to leave? Your Wife, Our Daughter, the fourth book in the seven-part Life Out of Reach series, begins with Jas trapped inside a marriage she has already ended in her own mind.

Jay has destroyed the last fragile possibility that a marriage she never chose might somehow become one she could live with and Jas wants to leave. Instead, her parents walk away and leave her behind.

Her husband wants his wife back and by his side. His parents believe marriage gives him rights over her. Her own family insists that whatever has happened, a daughter belongs with her husband.

Again and again, the people who should protect Jas become the very people keeping her in place. As Jay’s determination to hold onto her becomes increasingly possessive and controlling, the marriage descends into sexual violence, physical intimidation and psychological torment.

Jas responds in the only ways still available to her — anger, defiance, silence and a growing refusal to perform the role of obedient wife. Then motherhood changes the shape of her survival.

Her child becomes the centre of a small world Jas builds inside the captivity of her marriage. She continues her education, clings to her books and begins quietly imagining a future beyond the house, beyond Jay and beyond the families who believe they can decide where she belongs.

But leaving proves far harder than anything that Jas can imagine and a forced marriage turns into pursuit and captivity - because Jay doesn’t simply believe that Jas is his wife – he believes she is his to keep.
